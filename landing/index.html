Frequently asked

Why invite-only?

Because we're launching one city at a time, and the first cohort sets the temperature of everything that follows. We'd rather start with 2,500 obsessed players than 250,000 lurkers. Invite-only filters for intent.

Where do I get an invite code?

From a creator partner — fitness, running, lifestyle and culture creators across London have unique codes. They choose who gets in. If you don't have one you can still join the waitlist, but you'll be released in later cohorts after Cohort 01 is full. Or pay to skip the queue.

What do paid tiers actually buy me?

Priority access only. The £29 Priority Pass jumps you to the top 500 on launch day. The £99 Founder Seat is one of only 100 ever — with a permanent badge, a lifetime 2× coin multiplier, and access to the Founders' Vault. Both come with a launch-day starter chest of in-app items. Paid tiers do not buy lottery entries or real-money rewards.

What's in the Founders' Vault?

If we told you, it wouldn't be the Founders' Vault. What we'll say: it's permanent, it's exclusive, it can't be earned by playing — only claimed by being one of the first 100.

What rewards are coming?

We're signing partners now — fitness, food, retail, culture, all London-first. We're keeping the names sealed until launch, partly to protect the surprise, partly because the early players will get first crack before anyone else. Reward types confirmed: vouchers, exclusive product drops, experience access, in-app cosmetics, leaderboard prize pools.

Refund policy?

Full refund if Cohort 01 doesn't launch in 2026. We're shipping — but you're protected if we don't.

I'm a creator. How do I get a code to share?

Email creators@stridequest.app with your handle and following. We're onboarding a small first wave of London-based creators. Codes get personalised dashboards: track signups, conversions and rewards in real time.

When does the app launch?

London soft launch is targeted for the next quarter. Cohort 01 will be invited first, in waves. Public download opens after Cohort 01 is full and the city is stress-tested.
