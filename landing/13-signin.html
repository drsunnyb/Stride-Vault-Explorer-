How dashboard access works

NO PASSWORDS · EVER

The short version

Passwords are friction. People forget them, hate making them, and recover them via email anyway. So we skipped the middle step.

Three ways to get into your dashboard

  1. Welcome email — when you finish onboarding, your first dashboard link comes by email. Tap it, you're in.
  2. This page — drop your email, get a fresh link. Use it from any device, any browser.
  3. Stay signed in — once you tap a link, your device stays signed in for 30 days. No need to keep going to email.

What we send and when

  • Right after signup: Welcome email with your dashboard link + share kit
  • When someone joins via your link: "Maya from Hackney just joined — you're now #244" (capped at 3/day so it doesn't get spammy)
  • Weekly: Insider drop — borough race standings, position movement, paid-tier-only intel for Stride+ members
  • When Cohort 01 starts opening: "You're up. Download window opens for you in 24h."
  • When something matters: Hackney overtakes Brixton, you hit a milestone, a Founder spot opens. No noise — only signal.

If you change email or lose access

DM Sunny → he confirms it's you → we move the queue position to your new email. No drama. hello@stridequest.app
